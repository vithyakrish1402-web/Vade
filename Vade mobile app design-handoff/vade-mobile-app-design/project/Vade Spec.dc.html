A monochrome messenger with one green signal.

Built on the Organic system's structure — its spacing steps, radius scale, pill controls and Lucide 2.75 icons — with the palette and display voice retuned to the brief: near-monochrome surfaces, one restrained emerald, Figtree throughout. Emerald appears only where privacy state is being reported: verification, reveal, and the unread count.
